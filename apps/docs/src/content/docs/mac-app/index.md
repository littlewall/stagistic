The Mac app is Stagistic Editor installed on your Mac. It is free, needs no account and keeps scripts on your Mac. It works offline, backs up your scripts automatically and can connect to Claude and other AI apps through a read-only MCP connection.

It needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. On other computers, [use the Web app](https://editor.stagistic.app) in a browser.
