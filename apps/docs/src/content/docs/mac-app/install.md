## Check your Mac

The Mac app runs on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. To check, choose **Apple menu ▸ About This Mac**: **Chip** shows an Apple chip and **macOS** shows version 15 or higher.

On an Intel Mac or an older macOS, use the [Web app](https://editor.stagistic.app) in a browser instead.

## Download and install

1. Open [stagistic.com/download](https://stagistic.com/download) and choose **Download for Mac**. The file is `Stagistic-Editor-mac-arm64.dmg`.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. Drag **Stagistic Editor** onto **Applications**.
4. Open **Stagistic Editor** from Applications or Launchpad.

If macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet, choose **Open**.

The download link always points to the current Release. After the first install, the app [updates itself](/mac-app/updates/); you do not download it again.

## First start

The app opens on your script list, empty if this Mac has no scripts yet. Choose **New script** to begin, or [move scripts from the Web app](/mac-app/move-from-web-app/).

Scripts are saved on this Mac as you write. Close the window or press <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⌘</span> Command</kbd> + <kbd>Q</kbd> to quit: the app finishes saving and takes a backup first. See [Where your data lives](/mac-app/where-data-lives/).

## If the app does not open

- Check that this Mac has Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.
- Download the current Release again from [stagistic.com/download](https://stagistic.com/download) and replace the app in Applications. Your scripts stay: they are not stored inside the app.
- Choose **Help ▸ Report a Problem…** once the app opens, or open [feedback.stagistic.com](https://feedback.stagistic.com).
