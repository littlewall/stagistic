---
title: Format your text
description: Use bold, italic or underline to emphasize words in your script.
---

Use text formatting to emphasize particular words. For example, underline `moment` in Alex's line, `Give me a moment.` The block remains **Dialogue**.

## Format selected words

1. Select the words you want to format in the script.
2. In the editor toolbar, choose the formatting you need:
   - <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/bold.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Bold**.
   - <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/italic.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Italic**.
   - <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/underline.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Underline**.
3. Continue editing the script. You can combine the three styles on the same text.

You can also use the [formatting shortcuts](/editor/writing/shortcuts/#format-text) with the text selected.

## Remove a style

Select the formatted words and choose the same formatting button again to turn that style off. For example, select `moment` and choose <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/underline.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Underline** again.

If you applied formatting by mistake, use the editor's undo shortcut: <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⌘</span> Command</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> on macOS, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows/Linux.

## Text formatting and block appearance

The type of a block can supply its own appearance. For example, Aside text is italic with the default settings. Changing a word's formatting and changing a block's default appearance are separate operations.

Use [Change block type](/editor/writing/blocks/) when the text has a different role in the script. For the overall printed layout, see [Pages and export](/editor/export/).
