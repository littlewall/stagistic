Each part of your script has a block type. A **Character** block identifies the speaker, **Dialogue** contains their words, and **Stage direction** describes what happens on stage. The type also determines what happens when you press <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⏎</span> Enter</kbd> or <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⇥</span> Tab</kbd>.

You can choose a type before writing or change a block you have already written. The guides below follow a short exchange: Robin opens the door, speaks, adds a quiet remark, and Alex replies.

## What would you like to do?

- [Choose and change a block type](/editor/writing/blocks/) — give each part of the text its role.
- [Continue writing](/editor/writing/flow/) — move to the next speaker, continue the same speech or split existing text.
- [Customize your writing flow](/editor/writing/customize-flow/) — choose what comes next and assign your own number shortcuts.
- [Write dialogue and asides](/editor/writing/dialogue/) — add `quietly` between two parts of Robin's speech and return to the dialogue.
- [Write stage directions](/editor/writing/stage-directions/) — describe `Robin opens the door.` and adjust its indentation.
- [Format your text](/editor/writing/formatting/) — emphasize words without changing the block type.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/editor/writing/shortcuts/) — find the keys for a specific operation.

Each guide can be read on its own. For choosing speakers and managing their details, see [Characters](/editor/characters/). For songs, see [Music and lyrics](/editor/music/); for the structure of the script, see [Acts and scenes](/editor/structure/).
