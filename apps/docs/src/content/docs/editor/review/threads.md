title: Reply and resolve comments
description: Continue a comment thread, edit its messages and mark the discussion as finished.
---

After changing Alex's line, return to `Could Alex sound less urgent?` and record `Changed the line.`

## Add a reply

1. Open the comment card in **Comments** by clicking its summary.
2. Write `Changed the line.` in **Reply**.
3. Choose **Reply**.

The reply is added to the same thread. Enter adds a line in this field. <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⌘</span> Command</kbd> + <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⏎</span> Enter</kbd> on macOS, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⏎</span> Enter</kbd> on Windows/Linux, submits the reply.

## Edit a message

For the first message, open the card's <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/script-actions.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **More actions** menu and choose **Edit**. For a reply, use its own three-dot **Reply actions** menu and choose Edit.

Change the text, then choose **Save**. Choose **Cancel** or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> in the editing field to leave the message unchanged. The same Command/Ctrl + Enter combination submits an edited message.

## Mark a thread as resolved

1. Open More actions in the expanded card's header.
2. Choose **Resolve**.

The thread remains stored and is labelled **Resolved**. If the panel is filtered to Open, it disappears from that view; choose [All or Resolved](/editor/review/comments-view/#filter-by-status) to find it again.

## Reopen a thread

In a resolved card, choose **More actions → Reopen**. It becomes open again, and its Reply field returns. Reopen a resolved thread when you need to add another reply.

## Delete a reply or a whole thread

- In a reply's **Reply actions**, **Delete** removes that reply only.
- In the card header's **More actions**, **Delete** removes the entire thread, including its replies.

After deleting a whole thread, the app shows **Comment deleted** with **Undo**. Use that notification action to restore the thread if the removal was accidental. This is separate from the editor toolbar's Undo for script edits.

Deleting comments does not delete the script text they refer to. Use Resolve when you want to keep a completed note available for later review.
