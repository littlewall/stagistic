---
title: Confirm characters
description: Understand confirmed and unconfirmed characters and add a character to your managed list.
---

When you write a new character name, you can use it in the script before adding its details. The Characters sidebar shows these names under **Unconfirmed**.

Confirm a character when you want to manage its name, color, outline or vocal details, or include it in a character group.

## Confirm a name from the script

1. Open the **Characters** sidebar in the editor.
2. Find `ROBIN` under **Unconfirmed**.
3. Use the <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/confirm.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Confirm ROBIN** button at the right of the row.

After saving, Robin moves out of Unconfirmed into the confirmed character list. Use the pencil button, <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/manage.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Manage ROBIN**, to open the character's details.

You can keep writing with unconfirmed names. Confirmation is not required to finish a speech or save the script.

## How the two states differ

| State | What it means | Where to work with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unconfirmed | A character name appears in the script without a confirmed character record. | The **Unconfirmed** section of the Characters sidebar. |
| Confirmed | The character has a record with editable details and can be selected as a group member. | The confirmed list in the sidebar and **Characters** in the attribute manager. |

Pressing <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⏎</span> Enter</kbd> or <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⇥</span> Tab</kbd> to finish a character reference completes the text you are entering. The **Confirm** action adds the character to the managed list.

## Create a character before writing

You can also add a character from the Characters sidebar or create one in the attribute manager. This gives you a confirmed character that you can reuse when writing. See [Manage characters](/editor/characters/manage/).
