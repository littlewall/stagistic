A **Scene** heading starts a scene. An **Act** heading groups the scenes that follow it, up to the next act. You can work with scenes without adding acts.

For example, Robin and Alex leave the house in `The departure`, then come back in `The return`. Each act can contain several scenes, such as `At the door`, `Outside` and `Back inside`.

## A heading changes the structure

Scene and Act are block types, rather than text formatting. Moving a scene moves its content too. Removing a scene heading preserves the writing, but removes that scene's own details.

For general block types, see [Writing your script](/editor/writing/). For the appearance of headings and printed pages, see [Pages and export](/editor/export/).
