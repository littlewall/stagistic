---
title: Change or remove a heading
description: Remove a structural boundary while keeping the writing that follows it.
---

You decide that `Outside` should continue the previous scene. Remove its heading to join the writing, or turn the heading into ordinary text if you want to keep those words.

## Delete a scene heading

1. Place the cursor in the Scene heading you want to remove.
2. Beside the block, open <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/block-actions.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Block actions**.
3. Choose <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/remove.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Delete scene heading**.
4. Read the confirmation, then choose **Delete heading**.

The heading is removed. Its following writing stays and belongs to the previous scene. The removed scene's synopsis and place assignments are removed too; the shared places themselves remain available.

This action removes the boundary, rather than deleting the scene's dialogue and directions. Ordinary deletion keys protect scene boundaries, so use the explicit action when you want to remove one.

### Why is the delete action missing?

The first Scene heading cannot be deleted through this action. It anchors the opening scene. You can edit its title instead.

### Delete from the attribute manager

Use <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/attribute-manager.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Open structure in attribute manager** at the top of the Structure sidebar. Select the scene, then use the <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/remove.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Delete scene heading** button and confirm **Delete heading**.

It has the same effect and the same restriction on deleting the first heading.

## Keep the heading's words as ordinary text

1. Place the cursor in the Scene heading.
2. Open **Change block type** in the toolbar.
3. Choose **Stage direction**, or another writing type that fits the text.
4. Confirm **Convert heading** when asked.

The heading's words remain as the chosen block type. The scene boundary disappears, and its synopsis and place assignments are removed. Use **Cancel** in the confirmation if you want to keep the scene unchanged.

For example, change `Outside` into the Stage direction `Outside, Robin waits for Alex.` after conversion. The words now describe action within the surrounding script instead of starting another scene.

## Remove an act heading

In the **Structure** sidebar, find the act and use the <span aria-hidden="true">×</span> **Delete act** button beside its name.

Only the Act heading is removed. Its scenes and writing stay in their current order. They join the preceding act, if one exists; otherwise they appear without an act above them. This button performs the removal immediately.
