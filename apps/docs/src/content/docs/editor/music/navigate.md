---
title: Find music in your script
description: Use the music list, numbers and rail actions to find a musical span.
---

Use the **Music** sidebar to return to `Time to go` without searching through all of Robin's lines.

## Go to the start

1. Open the Music sidebar.
2. Find the placed number in the main list.
3. Click its numbered title.

The editor focuses the block where it starts. Entries under **Unassigned** have no starting location to navigate to; [place the number](/editor/music/add/#place-an-existing-number) first.

## Go to the ending

Click the music title pill to reveal its controls, then use <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/music-end.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Go to music end** when available.

The rail's start-marker menu also offers **Go to effective end**. From its ending-marker menu, **Go to start** takes you back.

The effective end is where the span currently finishes, whether set explicitly or derived from the surrounding script. See [Choose where music plays](/editor/music/range/).

## Read the numbers

Music numbering follows the scene containing each start and the order of starts in that scene:

| Music in a scene | Example in Scene 2 |
| --- | --- |
| One number | `2)` |
| Two numbers | `2.A)` and `2.B)` |

The number is separate from the title you enter. Adding another number in a scene can change its existing label from a number alone to a numbered letter. Moving scenes or music starts changes these labels to match their new positions.

## Find a number waiting for use

Look under **Unassigned** in the sidebar. To search a longer list, use <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/attribute-manager.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Open music in attribute manager**, then **Search music** in the manager's Music view.

The manager includes both placed and unassigned numbers. Selecting one there opens its details; use the editor sidebar to navigate to its starting block.
