---
title: Manage musical numbers
description: Create numbers in advance and edit their names, types and associated score.
---

Prepare `Time to go` before writing its scene, or rename a number already placed in the script.

## Create a number in advance

1. In the **Music** sidebar, use <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/add.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Add music**.
2. Enter `Time to go` in **Music title**.
3. Choose **Song** or **Instrumental** under **Type**.
4. Choose **Add music**.

The new number appears under **Unassigned**. To use it in the script, [select it through `#` in a Stage direction](/editor/music/add/#place-an-existing-number).

In the attribute manager's Music view, <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/add.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Create music** opens the same creation dialog. Creating a catalog entry there also leaves it unassigned.

## Open a number's details

Use the pencil beside its sidebar entry, <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/manage.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Manage music**. For `Time to go`, its accessible label is **Edit Time to go**.

You can also use <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/attribute-manager.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Open music in attribute manager** at the top of the sidebar, then select the number in **Music**. The title pill's controls offer **Manage music** too.

## Rename a number

1. Select it in the manager.
2. Change **Name** to `Time to leave`.
3. Press <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⏎</span> Enter</kbd> or leave the field to finish editing.

The linked music title in the script updates. Its placement and score stay associated with the same number. Ordinary words in prose, such as `Robin sings Time to go.`, are separate from the linked music title.

You can also edit the name directly in its music pill. Press <kbd><span aria-hidden="true">⏎</span> Enter</kbd> or leave its title field to finish editing.

## Choose Song or Instrumental

In the manager, change **Type**:

- **Song:** use for a number containing singing.
- **Instrumental:** use for music without sung words.

The type changes the number's classification and icon. It does not convert existing Dialogue or Lyrics blocks. [Choose their types](/editor/music/lyrics/) to describe spoken and sung text.
