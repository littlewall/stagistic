---
title: Attach a score PDF
description: Upload a PDF score for a musical number, preview it and replace or remove it.
---

Attach `time-to-go-score.pdf` to `Time to go` so its score is kept with the number.

## Upload a score

1. Open the number's <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/manage.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Manage music** control in the Music sidebar.
2. In its details, find **Integrated score**.
3. Choose **Upload PDF** and select the PDF file.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.

The filename and size appear in the score slot. Each number has one Integrated score PDF slot.

## Preview the score

Click the attached filename. The app opens its PDF preview. Close the preview to return to the number's details.

## Replace the score

Choose **Replace PDF** and select the revised file. The slot now uses that PDF for the number. Keep any older version you need as a separate file before replacing it.

## Remove the attached PDF

1. Choose **Remove** in the Integrated score slot.
2. Check the filename in the confirmation.
3. Confirm **Remove**.

The dialog removes the file from this browser and states that the removal cannot be undone. The musical number and its placement remain. You can upload another PDF afterward.

## Take the score to another device

[Download a `.stepkg` package](/editor/scripts/download/#download-a-package-for-backup-or-transfer) to keep the script with its associated attachments. The `.stagistic` text format does not include the PDF attachment.

## Include it in printed output

Uploading the score associates it with the number. For preparing output with integrated scores, see [Pages and export](/editor/export/).
