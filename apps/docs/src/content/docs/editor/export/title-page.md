title: Prepare the title page
description: Add the script title, credits, contact details, a logo and the date of your draft.
---

Before sending `The last door`, give its readers the author and draft details they need to identify the copy.

## Add the title and credits

1. In the app header, use <img class="kb-editor-icon" src="/icons/editor/settings.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true" /> **Open script settings**.
2. Choose **Title page** in the settings sidebar.
3. Check **Title** and, if needed, add a **Subtitle**.
4. In the credits table, enter a **Credit**, such as `Written by`, and the **Author(s)**, such as `Alex Morgan`.
5. Use **Add credit row** for another contribution, such as `Music by`. Within an Author(s) cell, **Add author** adds another person to the same credit.
6. Add **Source**, **Copyright** and **Contact** where needed.
7. Choose **Close settings**, open **Export** and [check the title page in the PDF preview](/editor/export/pdf/).

These details are saved automatically with the script. The Title field is also the script's name: changing it changes the name shown in the app and used for the PDF filename.

## Choose the draft date

Under **Draft Date**, choose the **Date format** and decide what the date should represent.

- Leave **Automatic date of export** on when the title page should show the date on which you prepare the output.
- Turn it off and choose **Date** when this draft needs a fixed date, even if you export it again later.

The **Preview** shows the date in the chosen format. The same draft-date choice supplies **Draft date** when you insert it in a [header or footer](/editor/export/headers-footers/).

## Add or replace a logo

In **Logo**, choose **Upload image** and select a PNG or JPEG up to 2 MB. The logo appears above the title in exports.

For an existing logo, use **Replace image** to choose another file or **Remove** to leave the title page without it. If an image is rejected, check the format and file size shown by the field.

## Need a cast list or contents?

The title page holds the details you enter about this script. Cast, place, contents and vocal-range pages are prepared from the script's structured information; [add them in Export](/editor/export/initial-pages/).
