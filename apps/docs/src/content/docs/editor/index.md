## Writing with Stagistic

The [user guide](/editor/getting-started/) follows the work of writing a theatre or musical script. You do not need to learn the text format to use the editor.

- [Scripts and files](/editor/scripts/) — creating, organizing and keeping your work.
- [Writing your script](/editor/writing/) — dialogue, stage directions and the flow between them.
- [Acts and scenes](/editor/structure/) — organizing and navigating the script.
- [Characters](/editor/characters/) — speakers, shared dialogue and references to characters.
- [Music and lyrics](/editor/music/) — musical numbers, lyrics and music references.
- [Finding and reviewing](/editor/review/) — search, comments and revisions.
- [Pages and export](/editor/export/) — layout, title pages and output for rehearsal.
