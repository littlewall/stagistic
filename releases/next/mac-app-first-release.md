- **Updates**: the app checks for new Releases, downloads them in the background and offers a restart in the footer. It never restarts by itself. Settings ▸ About app shows your Release and the update status.
- **Backups**: every 15 minutes when something changed, and before an update changes your database. Settings ▸ Storage lists them; restore one, or a backup file from another Mac, with Restore from File…. Attachments are not part of backups.
- **Safe closing**: closing the window, quitting and restarting to update wait until your latest changes are saved.
- **Mac menus**: a Script menu with Script Settings…, Attribute Manager…, Export… and Save Script…, plus Help links to the docs and the backups folder.
