Stagistic Editor is now also a Mac app. Your scripts stay on your Mac and are locally backed up automatically. It is free and needs no account, like the Web app. [Get it at stagistic.com/download](https://stagistic.com/download) (Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later). To move a script, download it as a `.stepkg` file in the Web app and import it in the Mac app.
