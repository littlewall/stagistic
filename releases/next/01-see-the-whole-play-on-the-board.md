The Board sits next to Editor and Export and always matches your script. Four lenses show it from different sides:

- **Wall**: scenes by act as cards, with a short outline and the characters in each scene.
- **Matrix**: characters against scenes, so you see who meets whom and where someone is missing.
- **Arcs**: each character's line across the play, rising when things get better and falling when they get worse.
- **Rundown**: the show top to bottom, with page ranges, characters, songs and their singers.

Add a scene on the Board and it appears in your script as a scene heading.
